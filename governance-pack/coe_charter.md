# AI Centre of Excellence — Charter (one page)
**Mandate:** standards, platform, assurance, acceleration. The CoE is not a monopoly on building.
**Scope:** all AI (models, GenAI, agents, vendor-embedded AI).
**Decision rights:** decides — risk tiers, platform choices, go-live gates. Recommends — portfolio priorities. Tracks — all registered use cases.
**Funding:** platform + shared services centrally; use cases by sponsoring P&L, first-use subsidies possible.
**Forums:** Portfolio board (monthly) · AI Council (monthly, with 2nd line) · Platform review (fortnightly) · Community of practice (fortnightly) · Value review (quarterly, with Finance).
**SLA:** intake tier decision ≤ 5 working days (published monthly).
